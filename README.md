# Red Ink

A 1v1 sword duel in steel and ink, played in the browser.

**Play:** https://utkubayram61.github.io/red-ink/

Your blade swings where you drag. A parry needs two things at once: the right moment and the right angle.

- Five weapons, each with its own art: Katana, Longsword, Zweihänder, Rapier and Twin Blades
- Parries, perfect parries, feints, clashes, dodges, rolls, acrobatic flips (vault over your opponent) and shuriken
- Five finishers
- Six arenas, among them a storm bridge, a snow temple and a windswept rose garden
- A dojo with 16 lessons
- Play against the bot, or a friend online (peer to peer, just share the room link)
- Character customization: headwear, hair, beards, armor, colors
- Full color or classic ink-and-red look (toggle in settings)

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse | Aim your guard |
| Left click | Attack (drag while clicking to choose the side) |
| Hold left click | Heavy attack; keep holding until the ring turns gold for the weapon art |
| Right click / Q | Parry (press late for a perfect parry); during your own windup: feint |
| WASD | Move |
| Shift · Space | Dash · roll |
| T | Acrobatic flip (toward them up close: vault over their head) |
| C · R · E | Crouch · jump · spin |
| X | Throw a shuriken |
| G · Esc | Attack indicator · pause |

A single HTML file built with three.js. No install, no account.
